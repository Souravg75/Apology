# 💌 "Sorry My Beloved Sister" - Interactive 4-Page Web Experience

A responsive, heartwarming, and interactive website created to apologize to a beloved sister.

---

## 🌟 Features & Highlights

1. **Page 1: The Heartfelt Apology ("A Letter From My Heart")**
   - Interactive opening 3D envelope that unfolds a sincere apology letter.
   - Sibling appreciation flip cards (*Always There*, *My Best Partner*, *Irreplaceable*).
   - Floating pastel heart particle background.

2. **Page 2: Memories & Golden Sibling Promises**
   - Polaroid scrapbook frames capturing classic sibling vibes (*Partner in crime*, *Snack sharing*, *The Queen Sibling*).
   - Interactive checklist of 5 unbreakable sibling vows (no more snack snatching, remote control peace, listening with care, etc.).

3. **Page 3: The Big Question & The Runaway "No" Button**
   - **Playful Evasion Physics**: Whenever she hovers, moves near, or touches the **"No"** button, it comically slips away, jumping to random safe locations on screen!
   - **Teasing Mascot & Dialogue**: Displays hilarious brother-sister quotes with every escape attempt (*"Too slow! 🏃💨"*, *"Error 404: 'No' not found! 😜"*, *"Only YES is allowed! ✨"*).
   - **Growing "Yes" Button**: Every failed escape attempt enlarges the "Yes" button with glowing pulse animations.
   - **Dropdown Interception**: Choosing "No" from the decision dropdown triggers a shake animation and automatically snaps back to "Yes".
   - **Grand Redirect**: Clicking "YES" triggers a fanfare chime, screen-wide confetti explosion, and redirects automatically to Page 4!

4. **Page 4: The Official Sibling Peace Treaty & Privilege Coupons**
   - Signed & dated Peace Treaty Certificate.
   - 4 Interactive Redeemable Sister Privilege Vouchers (Free Ice Cream, TV Remote Domination, 1-Day Chore Pass, Midnight Snack Delivery).
   - Printable / Downloadable certificate.

5. **Built-in Web Audio & Customizer**
   - 100% offline Web Audio synthesizer for chimes, boing sounds, fanfare, and ambient background lofi music.
   - Customizer modal (click the ✏️ icon) to easily change Sister's Name, Your Name, and custom apology text.
